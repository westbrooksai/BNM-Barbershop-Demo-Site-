Privacy Policy

Sample policy for a demo site. Have the shop review this before launch. Last updated: October 2026.

The short version

We collect as little as possible. We never sell your information, and nothing optional loads until you say yes.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, phone number and message, so we can reply. Messages are stored by our host, Netlify, and deleted when no longer needed.
  • Cookie choice: saved in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again. It never leaves your device.
  • Analytics (only if you allow it): anonymous page views and device type, to improve the site.

Third-party services

  • Square Appointments and Square gift cards: booking and gift card purchases happen on Square's site under Square's privacy policy. We never see or store card details.
  • Google Maps: the interactive map only loads if you allow "Maps and embedded media" or press "Load map". Google may then set cookies.
  • Google Fonts: fonts are served from Google's servers, which receive your IP address.

Your choices

You can change your cookie choices at any time from cookie settings, or clear this site's data in your browser. To ask us to delete a message you sent, call (301) 555-0123.

The Chair

Built by a Barber

Marco Reyes picked up his first set of clippers at fifteen, cutting friends on his back porch. He spent the next decade behind the chair in shops across Baltimore and D.C., learning from the best and picking up something from every one of them.

He opened BNM to bring that craft home to Frederick: a bright, clean shop where every detail is on purpose and every client is treated like a regular from day one.

“Everybody who sits in my chair should leave sharper than they came in.”

Marco ReyesFounder & Master Barber

Good to Know

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is open. Saturdays fill up fast, so booking ahead is the surest way to skip the wait.

What forms of payment do you accept?

All major cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash. Tips can go on the card.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule free up to 4 hours before your appointment. Late cancellations and no-shows may be charged 50% of the service.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Absolutely. Kids Cuts (12 and under) are $25, and Luis is our go-to for first cuts and nervous clients.
